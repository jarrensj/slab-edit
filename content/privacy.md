# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

slab edit is a free web app for covering the serial numbers on photos of graded slabs and adding a watermark before you share them. It's made by kwaji (“we”, “us”). It's built so your photos stay with you: they're edited in your browser and never uploaded.

## Your photos

Photos you open in slab edit, by picking, dropping, or pasting them, are edited on your device, in your browser. They're never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

- When you copy, download, or share an edited photo, it goes only where you send it: your clipboard, your downloads, or the app you pick in your device's share sheet.
- Photos are cleared from the page when you remove them, or close or reload the page.

## Your watermark

Your watermark settings (the text, the logo, its opacity, and the layout) are saved in your browser's local storage on this device, so they're ready the next time you visit. They're never sent to us. To clear them, tap **Remove** in the watermark settings, or clear this site's data in your browser.

## Analytics

We use [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) to count visits and see which pages get used. For each page view, it records:

- the page, and the site that linked to it
- the time
- an approximate location (country, region, and city)
- the browser, operating system, and type of device (phone, tablet, or desktop)

It doesn't use cookies, store your IP address, or follow you across other sites. Visits are told apart by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, and we only see totals. Nothing about your photos or your watermark is included.

## Hosting

slab edit is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives the usual details your browser sends when it loads a site, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-ups.
- No ads, and no tracking or advertising cookies.
- We don't sell or share personal information.

## Other sites

Sites we link to, such as kwaji.com, have their own privacy policies.

## Children

slab edit isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

If what slab edit collects or stores changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
